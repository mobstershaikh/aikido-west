後援

Aikido should be open to everyone, and our fees are the same for everyone. The two ideas meet here. A sponsored place covers a year of training and federation membership for someone who could not otherwise train, selected privately by the dojo, with full dignity and no public announcement unless they choose it.

Sponsorship is arranged by application and conversation, not from a price list: the figure follows who the place is for and for how long, and we agree it with you before anything is signed. Sponsors receive a named place, an invitation to gradings, and the association with an Aikikai Hombu recognised dojo in the Kanetsuka line. Stories are shared only with the scholar's written consent.
