奨学

Aikido should be open to everyone, and our fees are not negotiable in public. The two ideas meet here. A sponsored place is £985: a year of membership at £950 and the £35 governing body licence, for someone who could not otherwise train, selected privately by the dojo, with full dignity and no public announcement unless they choose it. We ask sponsors for a thousand pounds and hold the £15 balance toward the following year's places.

For a cultural institution or a corporate responsibility budget the ask is small enough for one signature and large enough to matter: a thousand pounds funds one place for a year, and five places open an additional weekly class. Sponsors receive a named place, an invitation to gradings, and the association with an Aikikai Hombu recognised dojo in the Kanetsuka line. Stories are shared only with the scholar's written consent.
