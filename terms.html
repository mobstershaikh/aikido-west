Terms

Plain terms for training at Aikido West. They apply to every booking, pack, course and membership. Published October 2026.

条

The terms

Who you are dealing with

Aikido West, Masbro Centre, 87 Masbro Road, London W14 0LR. aikidowest@hotmail.com. Prices are in pounds sterling and no VAT is added.

A first class and drop-in classes

A booked place is a confirmed place. Cancel up to 24 hours before the class for a full refund. Later than that, or if you do not come, the fee is kept, because the place was held for you and a class is full at fifteen.

The ten class pack

Valid for six months from purchase. Classes are booked one at a time and the 24 hour rule applies to each. Unused classes are not refunded after six months.

The foundations course

Six weeks for three people. Paid once, in advance. A full refund up to seven days before the first session; after that the fee is kept, because the cohort cannot be refilled. If we cancel a cohort you are refunded in full.

Membership

Offered by invitation. Monthly membership is paid by Direct Debit on the first of the month, with one month's notice to end it, given by email. Annual membership is paid once and is not refundable after the first thirty days. Membership holds your place; it does not carry over classes you miss.

Your right to cancel

Because you buy online, you have fourteen days from a purchase to change your mind and be refunded, unless the class or course has already taken place in that time, in which case we deduct the value of what you have used.

The British Aikido Federation

After your third session you must join the British Aikido Federation to continue training. Its fee is paid to the federation and includes your insurance. It is separate from anything you pay us.

Safety and conduct

Aikido is a contact martial art. You train at your own risk, you must tell the instructor about any injury or condition that affects your training, and you must follow her instructions on the mat. Anyone who trains unsafely or treats a partner badly may be asked to leave, with no refund.

Adults only

Classes are for people aged eighteen and over.

Changes

If a class is cancelled by us you are refunded or rebooked, your choice. We may change these terms; the date at the top is the date of the version that applies.

Law

These terms are governed by the law of England and Wales.

Aikido West

Traditional aikido in Brook Green, in the line of Kanetsuka Sensei. A member dojo of the British Aikido Federation, recognised by the Aikikai Foundation, Hombu Dojo, Tokyo.
