Ten on the mat

十

Why ten.

Ten is the number at which one teacher can correct every person in the room within two hours. It is also the number that lets beginners train with senior grades rather than beside them. When the six member places are held we run a waitlist, and when the waitlist is long enough we open another class rather than crowd this one.
