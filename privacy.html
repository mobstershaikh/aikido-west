Privacy

Aikido West keeps the least it can, uses it only to teach you safely and to reply to you, and deletes it when you stop training. This page says exactly what that means. Published October 2026.

何

What we hold, and why

Who is responsible

Aikido West, Masbro Centre, 87 Masbro Road, London W14 0LR, is the controller of your information. Write to aikidowest@hotmail.com about anything on this page.

When you book a class

Your name, email address and mobile number, the date you booked, and your answers to the health questionnaire and the participation waiver. We use them to confirm your place, to contact you about the class, and so that the instructor can teach you safely. Lawful basis: the contract you make with us when you book, and, for the health answers, your explicit consent, which you give at booking and can withdraw by writing to us.

Your health answers

These are special category data under UK data protection law. They are seen only by the instructor, are never shared with anyone else, are never used for anything other than teaching you safely, and are deleted when you stop training with us or twelve months after your last class, whichever comes first.

When you write to us

Your name, email address and what you wrote. We use them to reply and for nothing else, and we delete the correspondence twelve months after the last message unless you have become a member.

When you become a member

Your contact details, your British Aikido Federation membership number and grade, your attendance, and your Direct Debit mandate, which is held by our payment provider and not by us. We keep these for as long as you are a member and for six years afterwards, because tax law requires it for payment records.

Payments

Card payments are taken by Stripe. We never see your card number. Stripe's own privacy notice governs what it holds.

Who else sees it

Nobody, apart from the instructor, the dojo's administrator, and the payment provider, and the British Aikido Federation for your membership and insurance once you join. We do not sell, share or trade your information and we do not use it for marketing you did not ask for.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies of its own and runs no analytics. The map on the contact page is served by Google and may set Google's cookies when it loads.

Your rights

You may ask to see what we hold, to correct it, to have it deleted, or to withdraw consent for the health answers at any time, by writing to the address above. We reply within one month. If you are not satisfied you may complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
